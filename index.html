Project 01 · Analytics & Data Science

HR Analytics: Dashboard & Attrition Prediction

A four-page Power BI dashboard built on the IBM HR attrition dataset. It moves from a high-level executive view to attrition drivers, compensation and career indicators, and employee satisfaction, with filters and navigation buttons on every page. Behind it, a Python notebook cleans the data, explores what separates leavers from stayers, and trains models to flag employees at risk. Its cleaned output with risk scores feeds the dashboard.

1.47KEmployees analysed
16.12%Attrition rate
0.83Model ROC-AUC
41%Attrition in High-risk group
  • 237 employees left; attrition peaks among the youngest age group
  • Attrition is highest for frequent travellers (24.91%) and employees living 16+ km away (20.67%)
  • Lowest job-satisfaction group shows 22.84% attrition vs 11.33% in the highest
  • In the notebook, overtime workers left at 30.5% vs 10.4% for everyone else
  • Logistic Regression reached 0.83 ROC-AUC and 0.73 recall in 5-fold cross-validation, ahead of Random Forest (0.80 / 0.40)
  • The High risk group had 41% actual attrition vs 4.2% for Low
